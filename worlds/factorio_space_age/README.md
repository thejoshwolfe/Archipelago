## LLM Policy

If your PR is LLM generated or LLM assisted, please let me know using the 0-10 scale described here: https://www.visidata.org/blog/2026/ai/ .
I am only interested in reviewing levels 0-5.

I do all my work at level 0; I am an AI vegan; I have never made an account with any AI company.
If your PR includes substantial LLM generated content, I still appreciate your contributsion!
I will probably rewrite it to suite my coding style one way or another, but don't let that stop you contributing!

Disclosing your use of AI will help me understand how meaningful seemingly odd decisions are in your code.
